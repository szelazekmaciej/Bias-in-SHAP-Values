# Bias-in-SHAP-Values
This study explores the application of Machine Learning (ML) and Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) in quality management for industrial manufacturing. Focusing on identifying relationships between key parameters that define product quality, we integrated the XAI-based metric as a supplementary component of the decision support system. The goal is to create a unified approach to represent the results obtained using the fusion of domain knowledge and ML methods, while being anchored in current quality management practices. 
The proposed workflow integrates Statistical Process Control (SPC) practices with ML model, XAI--based indicators, and domain--specific expertise for process optmisation and product quality control. 
In the research, we describe two related use--cases, first presenting a way to identify areas with the greatest potential for optimization. In the second use-case, we estimate the improvement of production parameters in the context of necessary modifications to control limits.
We conducted our study based on real--life data and decision support practices obtained through cooperation with our industrial partner.
